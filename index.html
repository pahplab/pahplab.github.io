Physical Activity & Health Promotion Lab · Kyung Hee University

A day has 1,440 minutes.
We measure how people move through them.

The Physical Activity & Health Promotion Lab develops accurate ways to measure everyday movement with wearable devices and accelerometers, then uses those measurements to show how physical activity, sedentary behavior, and sleep relate to health. Better measurement leads to better health promotion.
